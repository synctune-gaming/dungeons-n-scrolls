# How to play

Your goal is to survive three regions and defeat their guardians. Each floor has eight rooms: an overworld entrance, six shuffled encounters, and a boss. Travel alternates between roads, woodland meetings, and dungeon rooms.

## Make a choice

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Swipe up, scroll down, or Up arrow | Explore, approach a foe, strike in battle, or continue after victory |
| Swipe left or Left arrow | Talk or sneak; in battle, guard and recover 3 mana |
| Swipe right or Right arrow | Reveal or trade; in battle, dodge |
| Keys 1-5 or the grimoire buttons | Cast a spell |
| P, the tonic icon, or Drink in the satchel | Use a healing tonic |
| A, Refill, or Drink in the satchel | Use an aether vial |

The buttons below each room always show the exact action, stat, and cost for that gesture. Glowing scene markers open chests, start conversations, reveal enemies, or inspect inscriptions. A downward swipe does not backtrack. One continuous mouse-wheel burst makes one choice, even during an animation, so trackpad inertia cannot consume your run.

## Discover what waits

Unidentified rooms, including guardians, appear as question marks on the route. Future regions stay unnamed on the map. Approaching an enemy reveals it and starts a fight without automatically defeating it. Raising the lantern costs 2 mana and reveals its identity and weakness without starting combat.

A successful sneak leaves an unidentified enemy unidentified. Its map entry stays hidden, marked Passed unseen. A failed sneak hurts you and starts a fight.

## Your adventurer

| Class | Health | Mana | Strength | Dexterity | Wisdom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wanderer | 80 | 28 | 8 | 6 | 6 |
| Shade | 62 | 30 | 5 | 10 | 7 |
| Ironbound | 96 | 24 | 11 | 4 | 4 |

Strength increases strike damage and makes forced locks safer. Dexterity helps you dodge physical attacks, sneak past an enemy's guard, and bypass rune locks. Wisdom strengthens spells, wards, and healing magic.

Leveling requires `12 x current level` experience. Each new level grants 1 strength, 6 maximum health, 6 healing, 2 maximum mana, and up to 4 restored mana. Even-numbered levels also grant 1 dexterity. The maximum level is 10.

## Read the fight

Every combat action spends a turn. The enemy displays its next intention: a quick lunge, a crushing blow, a curse, a raised guard, or a recovery opening.

Strike damages the enemy and uses any counterattack opening. Guard reduces incoming damage and recovers 3 mana; blocking a physical strike prepares a counterattack. Dodge uses dexterity to avoid physical attacks, but cannot evade a curse. A raised enemy guard reduces physical strike damage, not spell damage.

Study enemy costs 3 mana and improves subsequent strikes. During a fight it also spends your turn. Drinking a tonic or aether vial in combat gives the enemy an action, so use openings, frost, or a ward when recovering.

Guardians cannot be escaped. At half health they enter a second phase with a different attack pattern and appearance. Damage cannot skip the phase change, even with an overpowered strike or spell. The transition grants a breath to read the new intention before it attacks again.

Defeating a foe awards gold and experience exactly once, then keeps the scene and its spoils visible. Choose Continue to advance. The final guardian must be defeated and the last passage taken to complete the adventure.

## Your grimoire

| Key | Spell | Mana | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ember bolt | 6 | Fire damage, plus burning damage on two subsequent turns |
| 2 | Frost bind | 8 | Frost damage; freezes two enemy actions |
| 3 | Storm lance | 8 | Heavy arcane damage through a raised guard |
| 4 | Arcane ward | 5 | A shield with `14 + wisdom` strength that depletes as it absorbs damage |
| 5 | Mending light | 7 | Restores up to `10 + 2 x wisdom` health |

Fire, frost, and arcane weaknesses add spell damage. Frost cannot be refreshed while the foe remains frozen. Mending light works outside combat too, but cannot be wasted at full health. Offensive spells and wards require an active fight; unaffordable spells do not consume a turn.

An aether vial restores up to 14 mana. It cannot be wasted at full mana. Wells refill mana, conversations can restore it, and new regions grant up to 8 mana.

## Chests, shops, and safe havens

Dexterity 8 or higher opens a chest directly. Otherwise, match the three visible runes from left to right. Each wrong rune costs 4 health and resets your sequence; the third mistake jams the lock and leaves the chest behind. Closing the puzzle lets you try another approach. Strength 9 or higher can force a lock safely; lower strength costs 6 health.

Chests award seeded gold and an unowned relic when one is available. Relics apply automatically: the iron charm grants armor and maximum health, the fleet feather grants dexterity, and the ember stone grants strength.

Shops sell tonics for 15 gold, aether vials for 12, and an iron charm for 30. Buying does not leave the shop; choose Keep moving when finished. A tonic restores up to 24 health and cannot be wasted at full health.

Talking opens a conversation. Ask for a rumor to gain wisdom and a clue, share the fire to restore health and mana, or buy a tonic for 20 gold. Responding continues the journey; closing the conversation leaves you in the room without granting rewards.

Some passages, chests, and wells have hidden inscriptions. Investigating commits a seeded bargain before you choose. Accepting costs 4 health and may grant strength, wisdom, gold, or mana; a curse can take 10 additional health. Declining costs nothing, but the inscription cannot be reopened to reroll it.

Wells restore health and mana. You can also sacrifice 5 health for strength, or offer 10 gold for additional healing. Leaving a defeated guardian opens the next region and restores up to 10 health and 8 mana.

## Start again

At zero health, the run ends. Defeating the Pale Warden completes the adventure. New adventure lets you choose a class and a fresh seed; it resets health, inventory, gold, and levels. Your best floor and completed-adventure count remain.

Character, inventory, grimoire, map, journal, sound, and gesture hints are available from the navigation or mobile controls. Dialogs support Escape and return focus to their opener. Animated torches, creature movement, sword strikes, and spell effects follow your device's reduced-motion preference. Motion pauses behind dialogs and in hidden tabs.

Progress, unfinished combat, and preferences stay in local browser storage. Older version-1 adventures migrate without losing their route, health, inventory, or records; the original save remains as a backup. There is no account, tracking, server, or cloud synchronization. Clearing browser storage removes the save. If storage is blocked or damaged, the game explains the problem instead of claiming to save.
