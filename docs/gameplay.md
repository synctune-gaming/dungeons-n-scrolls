# How to play

Your goal is to survive three regions and defeat their guardians. New floors have twelve unique rooms: an overworld entrance, ten shuffled encounters, and a boss. New adventures span 36 rooms, plus any side trips. Travel alternates between roads, woodland meetings, and dungeon rooms.

## Make a choice

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Swipe up, scroll down, or Up arrow | Explore, approach a foe, strike in battle, or continue after victory |
| Swipe left or Left arrow | Talk or sneak; in battle, guard and recover 3 mana |
| Swipe right or Right arrow | Reveal or trade; in battle, dodge |
| Keys 1-5 or the grimoire buttons | Cast a spell |
| P, the tonic icon, or Drink in the satchel | Use a healing tonic |
| A, Refill, or Drink in the satchel | Use an aether vial |

The live encounter fills the available viewport, including on small phones and after rotation. Only health, mana, the floor/room counter, and quick supply tools sit above the scene. Room copy and choices overlay the bottom; combat adds enemy health, its next intention, and spells only while needed. Branding, stories, gold/XP, and read-only history do not push the dungeon down a page.

The buttons for the current room always show the exact action, stat, and cost for that gesture. They stay above the bottom navigation, clear of phone safe areas, with at least 44px touch targets. Gesture hints toggle the swipe labels on these buttons. Glowing scene markers open chests, start conversations, reveal enemies, or inspect inscriptions. A downward swipe does not backtrack. One continuous mouse-wheel burst on the encounter makes one choice, even during an animation, so trackpad inertia cannot consume your run. Scrolling over a button does not make a dungeon choice.

Scrolling stories, past encounters, search, profiles, and chat does not make dungeon choices. Keyboard adventure shortcuts are active only on the timeline's live encounter; entering a name or searching never attacks a monster.

## Discover what waits

Unidentified rooms, including guardians, appear as question marks on the route. Future regions stay unnamed on the map. Approaching an enemy reveals it and starts a fight without automatically defeating it. Raising the lantern costs 2 mana and reveals its identity and weakness without starting combat.

A successful sneak leaves an unidentified enemy unidentified. Its map entry stays hidden, marked Passed unseen. A failed sneak hurts you and starts a fight.

## Your adventurer

| Class | Health | Mana | Strength | Dexterity | Wisdom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wanderer | 80 | 28 | 8 | 6 | 6 |
| Shade | 62 | 30 | 5 | 10 | 7 |
| Ironbound | 96 | 24 | 11 | 4 | 4 |

Strength increases strike damage and makes forced locks safer. Dexterity helps you dodge physical attacks, sneak past an enemy's guard, and bypass rune locks. Wisdom strengthens spells, wards, and healing magic.

Leveling requires `12 x current level` experience. Each new level grants 1 strength, 6 maximum health, 6 healing, 2 maximum mana, and up to 4 restored mana. Even-numbered levels also grant 1 dexterity. The maximum level is 10.

## Read the fight

Every combat action spends a turn. The enemy displays its next intention: a quick lunge, a crushing blow, a curse, a raised guard, or a recovery opening.

Not every ordinary monster is equally tough. A light foe may fall to a strong spell; grave brutes, slag sentries, and oath knights have more health and armor and need several tactical actions. Approaching reveals a foe and starts combat without dealing a free killing blow.

Strike damages the enemy and uses any counterattack opening. Guard reduces incoming damage and recovers 3 mana; blocking a physical strike prepares a counterattack. Dodge uses dexterity to avoid physical attacks, but cannot evade a curse. A raised enemy guard reduces physical strike damage, not spell damage.

Study enemy costs 3 mana and improves subsequent strikes. During a fight it also spends your turn. Drinking a tonic or aether vial in combat gives the enemy an action, so use openings, frost, or a ward when recovering.

Guardians cannot be escaped. At half health they enter a second phase with a different attack pattern and appearance. Damage cannot skip the phase change, even with an overpowered strike or spell. The transition grants a breath to read the new intention before it attacks again.

Defeating a foe awards gold and experience exactly once, then keeps the scene and its spoils visible. Choose Continue to advance. The final guardian must be defeated and the last passage taken to complete the adventure.

## Your grimoire

| Key | Spell | Mana | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ember bolt | 6 | Fire damage, plus burning damage on two subsequent turns |
| 2 | Frost bind | 8 | Frost damage; freezes two enemy actions |
| 3 | Storm lance | 8 | Heavy arcane damage through a raised guard |
| 4 | Arcane ward | 5 | A shield with `14 + wisdom` strength that depletes as it absorbs damage |
| 5 | Mending light | 7 | Restores up to `10 + 2 x wisdom` health |

Fire, frost, and arcane weaknesses add spell damage. Frost cannot be refreshed while the foe remains frozen. Mending light works outside combat too, but cannot be wasted at full health. Offensive spells and wards require an active fight; unaffordable spells do not consume a turn.

An aether vial restores up to 14 mana. It cannot be wasted at full mana. Wells refill mana, conversations can restore it, and new regions grant up to 8 mana.

## Chests, shops, and safe havens

Dexterity 8 or higher opens a chest directly. Otherwise, match the three visible runes from left to right. Each wrong rune costs 4 health and resets your sequence; the third mistake jams the lock and leaves the chest behind. Closing the puzzle lets you try another approach. Strength 9 or higher can force a lock safely; lower strength costs 6 health.

Chests award seeded gold and an unowned relic when one is available. Relics apply automatically: the iron charm grants armor and maximum health, the fleet feather grants dexterity, and the ember stone grants strength.

Shops sell tonics for 15 gold, aether vials for 12, and an iron charm for 30. Buying does not leave the shop; choose Keep moving when finished. A tonic restores up to 24 health and cannot be wasted at full health.

Talking opens a conversation. Ask for a rumor to gain wisdom and a clue, share the fire to restore health and mana, or buy a tonic for 20 gold. Responding in the dungeon continues the journey; closing the conversation leaves you in the room without granting rewards. Each traveler gives free help only once per run, shared between dungeon encounters and world visits. Paid trades still use gold.

Some passages, chests, and wells have hidden inscriptions. Investigating commits a seeded bargain before you choose. Accepting costs 4 health and may grant strength, wisdom, gold, or mana; a curse can take 10 additional health. Declining costs nothing, but the inscription cannot be reopened to reroll it.

Wells restore health and mana. You can also sacrifice 5 health for strength, or offer 10 gold for additional healing. Leaving a defeated guardian opens the next region and restores up to 10 health and 8 mana.

## Profiles, following, and stories

**Timeline**, **Search**, and **Profile** remain available in the bottom navigation during gameplay without resetting the run. **More** opens adventure tools, gold/XP, stories, settings, and past encounters. Past encounters are read-only: they cannot replay fights or rewards, and passed-unseen monsters remain unidentified. Search, profiles, chat, and side trips keep their normal scrollable layouts.

Profile shows your current class, stats, equipment, and journal. Edit your adventurer's name using 1-32 characters and choose a pixel-portrait style and color palette. These are cosmetic choices; changing class still starts a new adventure.

Search also lists six fictional adventurers. Open a profile to see their character and journeys, Follow or Unfollow them, or open their chat. Profile keeps your followed-player list; followed adventurers appear first in the timeline stories strip. Following survives reloads and new adventures.

Open **More** and tap a portrait to open that adventurer's stories. Previous, Next, and Left/Right keys advance the manual viewer; Escape or Close leaves it and returns focus to More. Stories also open from adventurer profiles, and link back to the correct profile. Every profile and story is explicitly fictional, not live multiplayer activity.

## Search and playable side trips

Search cities, NPC locations, markets, and adventurers by name or description. Places and fictional players appear in separate result sections. Cities and their travelers belong to regions; destinations beyond your current region cannot be visited yet.

Your first side trip unlocks after **five completed dungeon rooms**. Starting a trip consumes that opportunity, even if you leave without taking a reward. Complete another five dungeon rooms to unlock the next trip. Combat turns, ordinary scrolling, gifts, city exploration, and venue visits do not count.

Visit opens a playable city, traveler interaction, or merchant. Chart a city's streets for 2 XP once per city per run, meet its local NPC, or buy real supplies with your current gold. A city and its own venues belong to one trip; you cannot chain a different city into it to avoid the five-room gate.

Side trips do not replace the active dungeon room. You cannot enter during combat, an unresolved interaction, or an ended run. Choose Continue after a victory before leaving for a trip. **Return to timeline** brings you back to the same encounter, preserving the intentional health, mana, and inventory changes made while visiting.

The active visit, used NPC rewards, and five-room cooldown persist after reload and across floors. You can browse other screens and resume the visit. Starting a new adventure resets this run-specific travel state.

## Preset-only chat and useful gifts

Open chat from a fictional adventurer's profile. Select an authored greeting, good-luck message, encouragement, or thanks. There is no free-text composer, editable preset, or custom gift note.

Gifts are **always free to the giver**. They take no gold, items, or stats from your hero. The fictional recipient gets a bounded local boost, shown on their profile and in their story viewer; repeat gifts cannot stack unlimited help.

The first gift to an adventurer in a run can create a simulated return gift in that thread. At most three adventurers can send returns, and you can claim at most **three boosts per run**:

| Gift | Effect on your hero when claimed |
| --- | --- |
| Trail ration | Restore up to 4 health |
| Aether spark | Restore up to 2 mana |
| Lucky charm | Add 1 strength for the current run, up to the existing strength cap |

Claim gifts outside fights, unresolved interactions, and side trips. Full health or mana leaves the relevant gift pending for later instead of wasting it. Claiming changes your actual saved hero but does not advance a dungeon room or spend the dungeon's random state.

Messages, sent gifts, recipient boosts, and claimed receipts persist locally. Unclaimed returns belong to the run in which they arrived: New adventure marks old pending gifts expired, while preserving their history. No message or gift is sent to a real account.

## Start again

At zero health, the run ends. Defeating the Pale Warden and taking the last passage completes the adventure. New adventure lets you choose a class and a fresh seed; it resets health, inventory, gold, levels, gift boosts, and side-trip state. Your best floor, completed-adventure count, personalization, following, and conversations remain.

Profile, inventory, grimoire, map, journal, sound, and gesture hints are available from the navigation or mobile controls. Dialogs support Escape and return focus to their opener. Animated torches, creature movement, sword strikes, and spell effects follow your device's reduced-motion preference. Motion pauses behind dialogs and in hidden tabs.

The compact scene-top tools are tonics, satchel, and grimoire. Tap the floor/room counter to open Map, or open **More** for all adventure tools. Use **More > Settings** for sound and gesture hints. Extra tools pause encounter input; closing a tool opened through More returns focus to More.

Progress, unfinished combat and visits, social history, and preferences stay in local browser storage. Version-1 and version-2 adventures migrate without losing their current floor route, health, inventory, encounter, or records; the old save keys remain as backups. Older saved floors keep their original layout; newly generated floors use twelve rooms. There is no account, tracking, server, or cloud synchronization. Clearing browser storage removes the save. If storage is blocked or damaged, the game explains the problem instead of claiming to save.
