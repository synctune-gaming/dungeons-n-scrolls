# How to play

Your goal is to survive three regions and defeat their guardians. Each floor has eight rooms: an overworld entrance, six shuffled encounters, and a boss. Travel alternates between roads, woodland meetings, and dungeon rooms.

## Make a choice

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Swipe up, scroll down, or Up arrow | Explore, attack, open a chest, or continue |
| Swipe left or Left arrow | Talk, sneak, scout, or take a blessing |
| Swipe right or Right arrow | Search, trade, escape, force a lock, or study a boss |
| P, the tonic icon, or Drink in the satchel | Use a healing tonic |

The buttons below each room always show the exact action, stat, and cost for that gesture. A downward swipe does not backtrack. One continuous mouse-wheel burst makes one choice, so trackpad inertia cannot consume your run.

## Your adventurer

| Class | Health | Strength | Dexterity | Wisdom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wanderer | 80 | 8 | 6 | 6 |
| Shade | 62 | 5 | 10 | 7 |
| Ironbound | 96 | 11 | 4 | 4 |

Strength reduces the health you lose in a fight and makes forced locks safer. Dexterity helps you sneak past an enemy's guard and bypass rune locks. Wisdom improves rest with strangers and reduces boss damage after studying a weakness.

Fights resolve in one choice. Surviving an attack defeats the enemy and awards gold and experience. Escaping costs health without awarding combat loot. Sneaking may fail, with a health cost. Bosses cannot be skipped; you can drink a tonic or spend 5 health to study one before attacking.

Leveling requires `12 x current level` experience. Each new level grants 1 strength, 6 maximum health, and 6 healing. Even-numbered levels also grant 1 dexterity. The maximum level is 10.

## Chests, shops, and safe havens

Dexterity 8 or higher opens a chest directly. Otherwise, match the three visible runes from left to right. Each wrong rune costs 4 health and resets your sequence; the third mistake jams the lock and leaves the chest behind. Closing the puzzle lets you try another approach. Strength 9 or higher can force a lock safely; lower strength costs 6 health.

Chests award seeded gold and an unowned relic when one is available. Relics apply automatically: the iron charm grants armor and maximum health, the fleet feather grants dexterity, and the ember stone grants strength.

Shops sell tonics for 15 gold and an iron charm for 30. Buying does not leave the shop; choose Keep moving when finished. NPCs can trade a tonic for 20 gold, or share a story and a little rest. A tonic restores up to 24 health and cannot be wasted at full health.

Wells restore health. You can also sacrifice 5 health for strength, or offer 10 gold for additional healing. Defeating a guardian opens the next region and restores up to 10 health.

## Start again

At zero health, the run ends. Defeating the Pale Warden completes the adventure. New adventure lets you choose a class and a fresh seed; it resets health, inventory, gold, and levels. Your best floor and completed-adventure count remain.

Character, inventory, map, journal, sound, and gesture hints are available from the navigation or mobile controls. Dialogs support Escape and return focus to their opener. Motion follows your device's reduced-motion preference.

Progress and preferences stay in local browser storage. There is no account, tracking, server, or cloud synchronization. Clearing browser storage removes the save. If storage is blocked or damaged, the game explains the problem instead of claiming to save.
